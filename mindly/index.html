Is my data private?

Your notes are encrypted in transit and at rest, and they are never used to train AI models or sold to anyone.

Does Mindly work offline?

Yes. Capture and edit offline, and everything syncs as soon as you reconnect.

Can I import my existing notes?

You can import from plain text, Markdown and most popular note apps in a couple of clicks.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Cancel from settings and you keep Pro until the end of your billing period.
